About Us
Lucerne Chocolate is dedicated to crafting premium chocolates made from carefully selected cocoa beans and high-quality ingredients. Inspired by Swiss chocolate-making traditions, we blend precision, passion, and creativity in every piece. Our chocolates are thoughtfully created to deliver rich flavor, smooth texture, and lasting satisfaction. From classic favorites to modern creations, Lucerne focuses on quality, authenticity, and memorable taste experiences that turn everyday moments into delightful celebrations.

Lucerne Produces Chocolate Categories
Chocolate Blocks & Bars

Our solid blocks are engineered for professional, temper-free performance, delivering a reliable finish for all coatings and confections. The Premium Dark offers intense cocoa flavor, while the Signature Dark provides a superior, richer profile with higher cocoa ingredients. Available in consistent 1kg bars and 2kg blocks, they are the ideal base for high-volume ganache, glazes, and industrial production needs.

Chocolate Chips & Drops

Lucerne chips are perfectly bake-stable, holding their shape to deliver authentic cocoa flavor and satisfying texture throughout your baked goods. We offer a variety of sizes and cocoa concentrations in dark and white compound, allowing you to control the flavor and aesthetic of your products precisely. These versatile drops also excel at simple melting, detailed drizzling, and functioning as elegant decorative elements on any dessert.

Chocolate Spreads

Achieve flawless results with our high-fluidity coatings, specifically engineered for quick-setting and brilliant, eye-catching gloss on ice cream and confectionery. Our creamy, ready-to-use sweet spreads and glazes guarantee consistent, luxurious flavor and texture, simplifying filling, layering, and dipping processes. These time-saving, professional staples enhance product presentation across your entire dessert line while maintaining peak performance and stability.
