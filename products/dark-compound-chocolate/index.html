The Lucerne Premium Dark Chocolate – Intense Cocoa Profile is the flagship offering in the professional dark chocolate range. This product is meticulously engineered to provide the visual appeal and reliable functionality of high-end chocolate without the need for time-consuming tempering. The formulation ensures a brilliant, smooth surface and a clean, satisfying snap, setting a new benchmark for high-performance dark chocolate. The flavor is centered on a Deep, Pure Dark Cocoa intensity, distinguished by a highly Balanced Sweetness that supports the core cocoa note perfectly, delivering a Straightforward, Rich Chocolaty Intensity with a Smooth, Luxurious Mouthfeel. Formulated with superior vegetable fat, this 1 kg Solid Bar (BDC401) is designed for professional use, featuring high flow (4/5 Drops) for perfect enrobing and intricate moulding, and an optimal 18% - 25% Min. Cocoa Powder content for deep color and authentic flavor. The product's inherent stability and temper-free nature make it ideal for ganache bases, truffle centers, glazes, and dipping applications.
